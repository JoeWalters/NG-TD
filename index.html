How to Play

  1. Design the maze first: drag from the green top edge to the red bottom edge, then hit Done Path.
  2. Tap or click an empty tile to place a tower along your path.
  3. Creeps follow your path — build along it.
  4. Click a tower to upgrade, right-click to sell.
  5. Hit Start Wave to send the next wave.
  6. Keep Lives above 0 — leaks cost one life each.
💡 Frost slows creeps; Splash deals area damage — pair them for a frosty combo.
